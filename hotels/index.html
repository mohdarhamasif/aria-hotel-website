Questions

What hoteliers ask us

The short answers. Amariya can go into detail if you ask her.

What is an AI receptionist for hotels?

An AI receptionist answers your hotel's phone line like a trained front-desk agent. Amariya picks up every call, answers questions about rooms, rates and facilities, takes bookings and guest requests, and passes the call to your staff when a person is needed.

Which languages does Amariya speak?

Amariya speaks 30+ languages on the Professional and Enterprise plans, and 5 on Starter. She detects the caller's language from the first sentence and switches automatically.

Does Amariya work with my property management system?

Yes. Amariya works with Opera, Mews, Cloudbeds, Apaleo and other common PMS and booking tools, so she can check live availability, quote rates and book rooms directly — without OTA commission.

What happens when a guest needs a real person?

Amariya warm-transfers the call to the right member of your team with a short summary, or takes a message and routes it when nobody is available — for example at night.

How long does setup take?

Most hotels go live within days. You connect your PMS and forward your phone line; no hardware is needed. We tune Amariya's answers to your property before launch.
