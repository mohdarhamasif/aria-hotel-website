Questions hoteliers ask about an AI receptionist

What is an AI receptionist for hotels?

An AI receptionist is a voice assistant that answers your hotel's phone line like a trained front-desk agent. Aria picks up every call, answers questions about rooms, rates and facilities, takes bookings and guest requests, and passes the call to your staff when a person is needed.

Which languages does Aria speak?

Aria speaks 30+ languages on the Professional and Enterprise plans, and 5 on Starter. It detects the caller's language from the first sentence and switches automatically, with natural, warm voices.

Does Aria work with my property management system?

Yes. Aria works with Opera, Mews, Cloudbeds, Apaleo and other common PMS and booking tools, so it can check live availability, quote rates and book rooms directly — without OTA commission.

What happens when a guest needs a real person?

Aria warm-transfers the call to the right member of your team with a short summary, or takes a message and routes it when nobody is available — for example at night.

How long does setup take?

Most hotels go live within days. You connect your PMS and forward your phone line; no hardware is needed. We tune Aria's answers to your property before launch.

How much does Aria cost?

Starter is $299 per month for up to 40 rooms, Professional is $799 per month for up to 150 rooms, and Enterprise is custom-priced for groups and properties with 150+ rooms.
