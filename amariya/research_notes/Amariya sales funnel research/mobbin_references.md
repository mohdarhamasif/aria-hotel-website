# Mobbin references: Amariya sales funnel

Source: Mobbin web sections and screens, searched 2026-10-18. Every reference below was opened and looked at (preview resolution). Notes describe only what the image shows. Counts are by product, not by capture.

Brief recap: one long dark sales page for an AI voice receptionist (hotels, restaurants, clinics, dental). Parts: hero with talk-to-it demo, problem, how it works, what she handles, founding offer, FAQ, apply CTA (apply, then a 20-minute call). No testimonials yet.

## Patterns seen in two or more products (precedent)

- **The voice demo is one big pill in the middle of the hero.** Vapi, PlayAI; Giga uses a single "Talk to us" pill as its only hero CTA.
- **Problem told as one statement with a lot of empty space**, not a grid of pain cards. Ease, Mixpanel; Midday does the same in prose (https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/1066980e-92c0-4746-b748-07ec0b72ac26).
- **Numbers in a row split by thin hairlines**, small label per number. Resend, Shopify (https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/abc5f5b2-e03a-4295-ab8e-ed8d7741126c), Zipline (https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/6e233999-9583-4e82-b105-04aa479c985d). Resend is the only one that shows a source line.
- **Steps on a vertical line with numbered markers**, one step active. Dovetail, Railway, Ada (https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/1cab397d-d972-4422-b896-34ed3741cef1, a scroll-driven line with the active number lit).
- **Capabilities as a list or tabs that drive one stage**, instead of equal cards. Beside (tabs + phone), incident.io (list + visual), Mural (https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/94100049-8b7d-4b4a-a114-bde6f6dd71f5, list + canvas), Glide (https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/598c5205-72c0-4923-851a-90176d351902, checked list + visual).
- **One plan, one price, one card.** Reflect, The Content Architecture, Agentcard, Skillshare (https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/1402b3eb-5585-4634-8884-807faa1bd563), Function (https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/f5d5c740-7af0-4718-9893-3b8e80e39eed, price left and inclusions right inside one card).
- **The guarantee sits next to the price or right under the button**, not in a separate section. Agentcard (badge beside price), Superpower (https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/6116ce47-e74a-4afc-a67f-fa7496e8579f, small reassurance row under the button), HoneyBook (https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/0f227019-e479-41c4-84ad-3cf6c8bff53e, "Money Back Guarantee" seal under the plans), Surfshark (https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/3b9b3e92-0140-4145-b907-0320747a350a, guarantee line under each button).
- **Scarcity stated in plain words, no timer.** Oevra ("Only 200 available"), Sunday (https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/ada6310f-fa68-4870-82d6-3ca2b97e267a, "Join the Founding Family / Apply to the 2026 Beta" with "Invite-only" as an underlined fact).
- **FAQ split: headline left, accordion right, hairline dividers.** Giga, Linear, Dovetail, Twingate (https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/28fbc552-e881-4058-8050-decef6b063f2). Giga and Dovetail add a "still have questions" link under the headline.
- **Final CTA is a centered headline and a single button on dark.** Slash, Melius, FLORA (https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/34d18078-910f-46c9-8c6d-291d5b260440), Coda (https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/0fca63d1-842d-453b-82e7-810992646b45).
- **Explain the process next to the apply button.** Apollo (two captures), OpenPhone (https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/785395c6-a1e0-4b50-8c20-d07a15aef374, Apply online / next steps / Apply now), Railway (Apply / Build / Ship).
- **Serif headlines on near-black for a premium tone.** Giga, Rox, Slash, Melius, Railway.
- **Customer logo strip under the hero.** Vapi, PlayAI, Giga, Rox. This is common, but Amariya has no customers to show. Do not fake it; leave the space empty.

## Single examples (no precedent found; treat as ideas, not proven patterns)

- Orb with a speech bubble showing what the voice says: SpaceXAI only.
- A large glowing sphere behind the pricing block: Reflect only. (Close to Amariya's orb.)
- Problem list where each pain is tagged "Handled by [product]" and the last row is "Yours again": Beside only.
- A live phone number to call as the demo, with a transcript ending in a booked appointment: Beside only.
- Capabilities shown as greeting bubbles in several languages: Giga only.
- A "How it starts" meta row stating the call length and what follows: Cloudstudio only.
- Capabilities as plain typographic columns with a left rule: Analogue Agency only.

## Gaps

- Frosted glass was rare in these results. The closest are dark panels with a faint border and slight gradient (Railway's step panel, Giga's stat cards at https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/4b7ca144-327d-4a66-b75a-647cdd01171d). No strong frosted-glass pricing card or FAQ turned up.
- No dark-theme example combined a one-time price, a seat limit and a guarantee in one card. Those three parts are each backed by a different product above.
